An Introduction

Hi, I'm Shaun, known online as Mxblah (and occasionally known as some variation thereof when it's already taken), and this is the part where I try and summarize myself in a handful of easily-digestible paragraphs.

Pre-University

Like most people, I was a kid once. Unlike some people, I grew out of it.
I grew up in the suburbs around Appleton, WI, and moved to the Northwoods of Wisconsin midway through high school. This was a rather traumatic experience at the time, but it forced me to make new friends and discover new interests. I also got my two cats as a moving present, so I think it was a net win.
After high school, I went to Michigan Tech for college, up in the terminally-snowy Houghton, MI. I spent four and a half years in Houghton, working towards the reward of finally getting out of there and moving back to civilization. Oh yeah, and the degree that I eventually attained in 2020.

University

At Michigan Tech (or to use its full name, Michigan Technological University), I majored in Mathematics Secondary Education with minors in Chemistry Teaching and Media Productions. I had originally started as a Physics major, but as many students do, I changed my major five times before I ended up with one I liked.
I worked for the University's IT department during my entire time at Tech, and I learned a lot about computers, system administration, being a manager, and other neat things. It's due in a large part to that experience that I ended up hosting this website, which is not affiliated with Michigan Tech.

Post-University

After graduating in the middle of the Covid pandemic, I applied for a full time position at MTU and was hired as a Windows System Administrator there. I spent a few years there gaining experience (and money) while getting to train my own student employees (to make some use of my teaching degree).
Why didn't I use my teaching degree (and certification) to become a high school teacher like I had initially planned? Why did I go into IT instead? Well, the first reason was trying to teach during the pandemic and seeing exactly how much work it is. The second is, as you might expect, the compensation. As a system administrator hired directly out of college, I made more than some veteran teachers of 20 years. Money isn't everything, but it sure is some thing.
I moved to the Detroit metro area in 2022 and currently work as an Infrastructure Engineer for Ford Motor Company. I am certainly grateful for MTU and especially the folks in its IT department, but I have to admit I much prefer the civilization down here to Houghton's middle of nowhere.
