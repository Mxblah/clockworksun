What personal data we collect and why we collect it

Visitors

We collect no data from visitors to the website outside of standard server logs, which may transitively include information such as public IP addresses that connect to the Site.

Comments

We currently do not have a comment feature on this website. If one is added, this policy will be updated (2018-12-17).

Media

We currently do not have a media upload feature on this website. If one is added, this policy will be updated (2018-12-17).

Contact

If you choose to email one of the provided addresses, we collect and store the email address used to contact us, as well as any information provided in the message or contact form. This is done in order to efficiently respond to any comments or reports. Emails may be stored indefinitely to ensure we have context to respond to related correspondence.

Cookies

We do not currently set any cookies for visitors accessing the website. There may be cookies set through embedded content as described below. Those cookies are governed by the privacy policy of the site they belong to.

Embedded content from other websites

Articles on this site may include embedded content (e.g. videos, images, articles, etc.). Embedded content from other websites behaves in the exact same way as if the visitor has visited the website from which the content is linked.

These websites may collect data about you, use cookies, embed additional third-party tracking, and monitor your interaction with that embedded content, including tracking your interaction with the embedded content if you have an account and are logged in to that website. We will attempt to make it obvious when content is embedded as opposed to local.

Analytics

We do not analyze any data you provide us except in order to better provide or improve the Website’s services. No automatic analytics are performed with the exception of third party analytics if you choose to interact with embedded content as described above.

Who we share your data with

We do not currently share any data collected with any third parties, and will not share any data unless served with a legitimate US court order or similar legal proceeding.

How long we retain your data

We currently do not collect data from visitors, so we do not retain it. This policy will be updated if this changes (2018-12-17).

Where we send your data
